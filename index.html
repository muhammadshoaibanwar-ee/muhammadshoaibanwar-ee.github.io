Flagship Project 01 · Microprocessor Systems

Smart Home Security System — PIN-Protected Door Lock with Laser Latch

Most hobby lock projects stop at a buzzer. This one implements a complete three-state finite state machine on the ATmega328P: idle, entry, and lockout, with ascending PWM tones for access granted, descending for denied, and a 60-second hard lockout after three failed attempts. The laser diode is the latch mechanism. Fully modeled in Tinkercad before being assembled and physically stress-tested across 50+ consecutive entry cycles.

PlatformArduino Nano — ATmega328P @ 16 MHz
Interface4x4 matrix keypad + 16x2 I2C LCD (PCF8574)
LatchLaser diode — PWM-controlled, fail-secure on power-up
VerifiedTinkercad simulation + 50-cycle physical test (100% accuracy)
Arduino C++FSM Design I2C ProtocolPWM Audio TinkercadEmbedded
Flagship Project 02 · Electronic Circuit Design

High-Precision Active Peak Detector — Diode Error Reduced to Under 10 µV

A standard diode peak detector throws away ~700 mV to the forward drop. That is a measurement error before you even start. Enclosing the diode inside an op-amp feedback loop (TL082CD J-FET) reduces the effective error to under 10 µV. Measurement accuracy holds within 3% from DC through 10 kHz. Designed and validated in NI Multisim at 0.02% simulation error, then physically soldered onto Vero board and cross-checked against a calibrated bench DMM.

Op-AmpTL082CD Dual J-FET — ±12 V supply rail
Accuracy<3% error DC–10 kHz; 0.02% achieved in Multisim
ReadoutArduino ADC (10-bit, moving-average filtered) to 16x2 LCD
HardwareFully soldered Vero board — bench DMM validated
Analog DesignOp-Amp NI MultisimVero Board Signal Processing

Flagship Project 03 · Electromagnetic Field Theory

Wireless Charging Tile for Smart City — First-Principles Electromagnetic Design

No simulation package was used for the core design. This coil was derived analytically from the ground up using Biot-Savart Law and Faraday's Law. The derivation establishes that exactly 32 transmitter turns deliver 5 A or more to a receiver coil at 5 cm separation, operating at 100 kHz on a flat Qi-compatible spiral. A custom Python model (NumPy + Matplotlib) then generated three parameter-sweep graphs that independently confirmed the analytical result.

Coil32-turn flat spiral, 8 cm radius, litz wire, 100 kHz
Output5.04 A delivered at 5 cm — meets Qi specification
TheoryBiot-Savart + Faraday's Law — derived analytically
